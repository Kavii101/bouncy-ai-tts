# Narration and dialogue — verbatim

Record founder lines to camera; VO in the same voice where marked.
Interviewer and candidate are separate clean recordings. Do not invent a
project outcome for the candidate; fixture per bible §8.

**S01 · Founder (hook)**
"You can know your work and still struggle to explain it when the question
comes. I'm Kavodel, and I built Bounty Online co-pilot to help people
prepare around their own experience."

**S02 · VO (context)**
"Start with your CV and the job description. The assistant connects what
the role needs to the examples you have supplied. When something is
missing, that should stay visible."

**S03 · VO (brief)**
"Every requirement is linked to evidence from the résumé. Gaps are
flagged, never invented — and nothing downstream uses the brief until
you approve it."

**S03b · VO (practice)**
"Practice out loud. The question is read to you, you answer by voice,
and the feedback tells you exactly what to strengthen."

**S04 · Interviewer**
"Tell me about a time stakeholders disagreed about an important metric.
How did you reach a decision?"

**S05 · VO (cue)**
"A short cue, drawn only from your approved evidence — so you can start
speaking immediately, in your own words."

**S06 · Candidate** (fixture answer — adapt only to the approved example;
do not add outcomes)
"Product and finance were using different revenue definitions. I brought
both definitions into one document, we agreed the decision owner for
each metric, and we published a single model the dashboards now use."

**S06 · Interviewer (follow-up)**
"What changed after that decision, and how did you know it worked?"

**S07 · VO (gap)**
"And when your profile has nothing — like a Kubernetes migration you
have never done — it says so, and offers a truthful bridge instead of
inventing one."

**S08 · VO (debrief)**
"Now review the answer. Which parts addressed the question? Which claims
needed more evidence? Here is one thing to practise before the next
interview — and it is saved as your next action."

**S09 · VO (present bridge)**
"The same co-pilot turns your script into a teleprompter that follows
your voice, holds when you pause, and bridges you back when you wander."

**S10 · VO (listen)**
"And the reports nobody reads become voiceovers people actually finish."
