## The three costs (one per audience)

1. **The unread report (Listen).** Analytics and BI teams spend skilled
   hours producing dashboards and insight reports that stakeholders skim
   or skip. The artefact already exists; the consumption does not.
   Voiceover converts the same artefact into an absorbable format —
   commute, walk, between meetings — with no new content production.
   *Approved line:* "The reports nobody reads become voiceovers people
   actually finish."

2. **The lost interview (Interview).** Experienced candidates fail
   interviews they are qualified for because, under pressure, they cannot
   recall the right example or structure it in time. Generic AI answers
   make this worse: polished, unsupported, and detectable. Preparation
   anchored to the candidate's own verified-from-their-CV evidence, with
   gaps shown honestly, attacks the actual failure mode — recall and
   structure, not knowledge.
   *Approved line:* "You have the experience. Bounty Online co-pilot
   helps you bring it into the moment."

3. **The read-aloud presentation (Present).** Presenters read slides
   because speaker notes are static and scrolling tools run at fixed
   speeds. Attention goes to the text, not the audience. A prompter that
   follows the speaker's voice and re-anchors them when they drift
   returns eye contact and pace to the room.
   *Approved line:* "Deliver the talk, don't read it."

actions completed, brief-to-practice conversion, user-rated cue
usefulness, and grounded-cue proportion / unsupported-claim rate from the
evaluation harness (bible §8 fixture). Until measured, the film sells the
problem and the working mechanism — not results.
